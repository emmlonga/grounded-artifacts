## Handoff rules for the draft

1. Evidence is permission to use, not an obligation to render: the reader must leave each section understanding its essential propositions; use the available evidence where it explains, proves or makes a proposition concrete, and omit what does no reader-facing work unless a MUST INCLUDE obligation names it.
2. Use constants only in their accepted renderings; names only in their anonymized renderings; terms with their §6 audience rendering at first use. A guardrail's fact appears once, where it bites, in plain words; a definition or caveat planned in two sections carries the reader-facing reason the outline gives, or appears once.
3. A label, pointer, announced count or stated trade-off enters the prose only when it materially improves orientation, progression or comprehension for the reader, never because the planning artifacts contain one. The grounding's tracking terms (§6 forbidden phrasing) never reach the reader.
4. Caps and the guardrail are planning diagnostics; the preview render decides. A reading over either is reported, not repaired, once the preview passes.
